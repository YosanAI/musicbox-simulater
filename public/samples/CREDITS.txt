SOURCE CREDITS AND ARRANGEMENT LICENSE
=====================================

All underlying requested compositions are historical public-domain works.
Sources below identify consulted editions/encodings and their stated terms.
These assets contain newly made reductions/voicings, revised tempos,
mechanical pin spacing, and geometry representations.

The new arrangement contributions in this package are released under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The Blue Danube reduction incorporates the credited CC BY-SA 4.0 edition.
The Carmen adaptation incorporates the credited CC BY-SA 2.5 edition and
is shared here under CC BY-SA 4.0. Preserve the source credits and indicate
changes when redistributing adapted arrangements. The inherited Canon in D
attribution is preserved, including its CC BY 4.0 transcription credit.

The flutetunes.com references were consulted for the historical melody.
Their score artwork, recorded audio, downloaded MIDI files, and modern
piano accompaniment tracks are not included. These tunes use independently
authored accompaniment and timing.

The Python tools in tools/ are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
